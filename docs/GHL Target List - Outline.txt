GHL TARGET LIST — Governance Process Consulting
Ranked by population and confirmed/reported AI deployment
Excludes Atlanta, South Fulton, and Montgomery AL (already on the relationship track)

Caveat, flagged not smoothed over: Detroit, Cleveland, and Richmond have legally nonpartisan mayoral elections. "Democrat-led" there means the officeholder's personal party affiliation, not a partisan city-government structure like Atlanta or Chicago. Worth a decision on whether nonpartisan-but-Democrat-affiliated cities count for this vertical before they are loaded into outreach.


1. New York City, NY — Population 8.3M
   Political basis: Mayor Mamdani (D/DSA), sworn in January 2026
   Known AI deployment: MyCity chatbot — a documented failure. Gave illegal/dangerous advice, cost approximately $500K, shut down January 2026 by Mamdani. This is a publicly documented governance failure — the strongest pitch on this list.
   Buyer office: First Deputy Mayor Dean Fuleihan; Deputy Mayor for Operations Julia Kerson (confirmed current as of June 2026) — both oversee Environmental Protection, Parks, Sanitation, Transportation, and Emergency Management.
   Contact: Mayor's Office of Operations, 253 Broadway, 10th Floor, New York NY 10007. Phone: 212-788-1400. No published office email found.

2. Chicago, IL — Population 2.73M
   Political basis: Mayor Brandon Johnson (D)
   Known AI deployment: Formal "Roadmap for AI"; CTA multilingual chatbot. No confirmed direct city-hall resident chatbot — moderate confidence only.
   Buyer office: No city manager (strong-mayor system). Deputy Chief of Staff Penny Smith reported in role. Note: council/mayor budget conflict ongoing as of December 2025 — added political complexity.

3. Philadelphia, PA — Population 1.5M+
   Political basis: Mayor Cherelle Parker (D)
   Known AI deployment: Council held first AI-oversight hearing October 2025; officials admitted current safeguards (facial recognition, gunshot detection, drones) are inadequately documented. Strong fit — council itself is on record saying governance is weak.
   Buyer office: Managing Director Adam K. Thiel — direct city-manager-equivalent/COO, confirmed current as of February 2026. Note: Inquirer reporting flags him as low-profile amid scrutiny over absences and outside work — worth weighing for outreach timing.
   Contact: Managing Director's Office, 1600 Arch St, Philadelphia PA 19103. Phone: 215-686-1776. Website: phila.gov/MDO. No published office email found.

4. Washington, D.C. — Population 702K
   Political basis: Mayor Muriel Bowser (D); Democrat-majority council
   Known AI deployment: AI Values Statement/Strategic Plan signed 2024; chatbots reported as "coming," not yet confirmed live — more planning-stage than production.
   Buyer office: City Administrator Kevin Donahue — confirmed current (March/May 2026 council testimony).
   Contact: Office of the City Administrator, John A. Wilson Building, 1350 Pennsylvania Ave NW, Suite 513, Washington DC 20004. Phone: (202) 478-9200. Website: oca.dc.gov. Office email could not be confirmed (search returned a garbled string) — pull the literal address from oca.dc.gov directly before outreach; do not construct one.

5. Baltimore, MD — Population ~586K
   Political basis: Mayor Brandon Scott (D)
   Known AI deployment: Formal AI executive order (March 2024): bans sensitive-data uploads and AI impersonation, mandates disclosure, created an AI advisory committee — a governance framework already in place, a good retainer-upsell angle.
   Buyer office: Chief Administrative Officer Faith Leach — confirmed current. Khalil Zaied is Deputy Mayor of Operations (also Department of Public Works director per the FY2026 budget).
   Contact: Faith Leach's office phone (410) 396-0137; staff page baltimorecity.gov/mayor/staff/faith-p-leach. An email (faith.leach@baltimorecity.gov) surfaced via a third-party aggregator only — about 60% confidence, not officially confirmed, verify before use. Reliable fallback: Mayor's Office of Constituent Services, 410-396-4900.

6. Detroit, MI — Population ~639K
   Political basis: Mayor Mary Sheffield, registered Democrat — note: Detroit mayoral races are legally nonpartisan
   Known AI deployment: "Emily," an AI customer-service agent, live in a pilot (Districts 3-4) as of December 2025 — one of the most concrete operational deployments on this list.
   Buyer office: New COO Winnie Liao (started February 2026, oversees 17 departments) — strong target given her operations mandate.

7. Boston, MA — Population ~654-674K
   Political basis: Mayor Michelle Wu (D)
   Known AI deployment: Live AI-search tool for city-service Q&A; first US city with a formal generative-AI workforce policy. No incident or failure found — a "doing it right" angle, less urgent than NYC or Philadelphia.
   Buyer office: Chief of Staff Clare Kelly — closest mayor's-office equivalent found; no distinct administrator title surfaced.

   Buyer office: No city-manager-equivalent title surfaced yet — needs follow-up.

11. New Orleans, LA — Population ~390K
   Political basis: Mayor Helena Moreno (D), won the October 2025 nonpartisan primary outright; sworn in January 2026
   Known AI deployment: Strongest AI-deployment signal of the second research pass, with multiple confirmed systems — "Jazz," an AI-powered textable 311 chatbot; AI agents triaging 911 calls since 2023 (expanding to 311); "ChatNOLA," a civic chatbot for pothole and drainage reports; Boostlingo AI interpretation for council meetings. Also a reported undisclosed facial-recognition program causing civil-liberties backlash — an extra governance-exposure angle.
   Buyer office: CAO likely Joe Giarrusso — about 70% confidence, appointment not finalized as of search, needs a follow-up pass before outreach.

12. Pittsburgh, PA — Population ~300K
   Political basis: Mayor Corey O'Connor (D), won a partisan general election in November 2025 (beat the Republican nominee after a Democratic primary win over incumbent Gainey); sworn in January 2026. Correction: an earlier research pass mistakenly flagged this race as nonpartisan — it is not.
   Known AI deployment: None found in core city operations. The Housing Authority of Pittsburgh (a separate, quasi-independent entity) is piloting Bob.ai for recertifications (about $160K contract) and a Google Gemini pilot for 60 staff — about 60% confidence this counts as "city government" versus a distinct authority.
   Buyer office: COO Sharon Werner (named, former DOJ/Obama official); Deputy COO Claris Chang.

   Buyer office: No city-administrator-equivalent title surfaced. Rochester uses a strong-mayor structure — needs a follow-up search on charter staff titles.
